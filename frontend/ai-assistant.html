What does elevated NDCI indicate?
An elevated NDCI (Normalized Difference Chlorophyll Index) value — typically above 2σ from the seasonal baseline — indicates elevated chlorophyll-a concentrations in the water column, which is a primary proxy for cyanobacterial or algal bloom activity. The current Zone 01 reading of 0.42 is 3.2σ above seasonal mean, which qualifies as a SEVERE-level indicator.

What drives SPM turbidity in reservoir systems?
Suspended Particulate Matter (SPM) turbidity in reservoir systems is driven by three primary mechanisms: (1) runoff-induced sediment influx following precipitation events, (2) algal bloom-associated scattering from high phytoplankton density, and (3) wind-driven resuspension of bottom sediment in shallow zones. Zone 02 currently shows B04 (665 nm) reflectance consistent with mechanism 1.
