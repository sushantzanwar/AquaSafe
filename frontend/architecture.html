🛰 Physical Optical Radiometry
Utilizes 11 Sentinel-2 MSI Bottom-of-Atmosphere (BOA) reflectance bands. Evaluates Red Edge (705nm) to Red (665nm) ratios for chlorophyll-a estimation without expensive chemical grab sampling.
⚡ Sub-Second Edge Pipeline
FastAPI async request loops calculate water mask segmentation, multi-spectral indices (NDWI, NDTI, NDCI), and historical z-scores in under 80 milliseconds.
🔬 Grounded Scientific RAG
Zero hallucinations: Gemini is constrained by ChromaDB vectors containing EPA Clean Water Act criteria, WHO recreational limits, and peer-reviewed optical hydrology papers.
